Read how your GPX files stay private

Last updated 6 September 2026

Your GPX files

The comparison runs in your browser. GPX files, route names, results, and checklist choices are not uploaded or saved on a server.

Refreshing or closing the page clears the active comparison. Demo data uses a separate in-memory session and is never kept as your data.

Network requests and device storage

The app loads its own code and artwork from this product domain. It sends no GPX content to any network service.

The app uses no accounts, analytics, ads, cookies, tracking pixels, third-party scripts, or map tiles.

An offline cache stores the app shell on your device. Clear it with your browser's site-data controls.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider may process IP address, browser type, and request time for security and reliability.

We do not use this information to profile riders.
