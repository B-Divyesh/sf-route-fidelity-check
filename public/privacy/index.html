Privacy, by default.

Last updated 27 August 2026

Your GPX files

Route Fidelity Check processes GPX files entirely in your browser. Files, coordinates, route names, comparison results, and checklist state are not uploaded to us or stored on a server. Closing or refreshing the page clears them.

Network requests

The app loads its own code and artwork from route-fidelity-check.sociobot.in. It uses no third-party scripts, map tiles, analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking pixels. An offline cache may store the app shell on your device; you can clear it through your browser’s site-data controls.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider may temporarily process standard request metadata such as IP address, browser type, and request time for security and reliability. We do not use this information to profile riders.

Questions

Privacy questions can be sent through Sociobot.
