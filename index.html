    Method

    A neutral check between route engines

    1

    Read locally

    GPX tracks or routes are parsed in your browser and never leave your device.

    2

    Normalize density

    Both lines are sampled by distance, so extra track points do not distort the result.

    3

    Check both ways

    Each route is measured against the other to catch detours, shortcuts, and extra loops.
