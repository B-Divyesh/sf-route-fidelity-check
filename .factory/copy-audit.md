The headline has six words: “Compare planned and exported GPX routes.” The
demo label, headings, field labels, button labels, metric labels, and legend
entries are fragments. Each contains nine words or fewer and names its job or
action directly.

| Concept | One public term |
| --- | --- |
| Original route | planned route |
| App or device output | exported route |

Internal identifiers still use `intended` where changing the data model would
add risk. Visitor-facing copy consistently uses “planned route.”
